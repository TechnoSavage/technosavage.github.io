# My Thoughts on the TryHackMe "NoScope" controversy

In the wake of controversy surrounding the announcement of NoScope there have been many responses by members of the community. Some fairly tempered while others more extreme (though not unwarranted). One
thing I have yet to see is anyone defending the actions and tactics of TryHackMe and the newly announced and somewhat amusingly named "NoScope" automated AI pentesting platform.

For those who are not aware of what this is all about I will give a brief explanation but there are other sources that are far more comprehensive that I invite you to review.



TryHackMe extended a rather large discount for users who prepaid to extend their membership by another year. The timing of the discount, in retrospect, is interesting and I don't recall a time in the past
where such a discount was offered, though I could be wrong. It's hard not to question if the offer had an ulterior, calculated motive of retaining a number of the user base after the announcement of NoScope. This is pure
speculation on my part with no supporting evidence, save the lack of transparency that we have observed so far. In any case it may be indicative of the amount of trust TryHackMe has lost in this latest maneuver.
